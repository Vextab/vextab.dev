Privacy policy for Fare Game: Idle Taxi Company

Effective from 27 November 2026

In short: Your game is saved on your own device, and Android may back it up to your own Google account. We have no accounts, no server of our own and no statistics of our own about you. The game uses Google's services for ads (AdMob), consent and purchases (Google Play) – so some information about your device is sent to Google, including every time the game starts if you have not bought No Ads. Ads are shown only when you tap a button to watch one for a reward. If you have bought No Ads, the game does not contact Google's ad or consent services at all.

Who is responsible?

The game Fare Game: Idle Taxi Company is developed by Vextab AB (reg. no. 559469-5446), Sweden, which is the data controller for the game. Contact: support@vextab.dev.

Google is an independent controller for what Google processes for ads, consent, purchases and backups (see below).

Your game

Everything about your taxi company – money, cabs, drivers, upgrades, goals, purchases and settings – is stored locally in the game on your device. The game has no account, no sign-in and no leaderboards, and we never receive your game data.

Android backup

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may save a copy of the game's save data in your own Google account (Google's backup). This lets your progress move to a new phone or come back if you reinstall the game. The backup is handled by Android and Google under their terms – Vextab never has access to it. You control it in your phone's settings (usually under Google → Backup).

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game can remind you when your cabs have stopped or when the offline limit has been reached. The reminders are scheduled and shown by your own device – no information is sent to us or anyone else. You can turn them off under Settings in the game or in your phone's settings.

Ads and consent (Google AdMob)

The game never shows ads on its own. All ads are optional rewarded ads: an ad is shown only when you tap a button to watch one for a reward, such as double earnings, a temporary Boost or a free service.

Ads are delivered by Google AdMob, and consent is handled by Google's consent service, the User Messaging Platform (Google Ireland Limited). This is how the game uses them:

  • Every time the game starts, if you have not bought No Ads, it asks Google's consent service what applies to you. This sends information about your device and your IP address, among other things, to Google. It happens even if you never watch an ad.
  • In the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland (where consent is required), Google's consent form is shown only when you tap an ad button yourself. Until you have answered, Google's ad library is not started and no ad is loaded.
  • Outside those areas, or once you have answered the consent form, the game starts Google's ad library when the game starts and loads a rewarded ad in advance, so that it is ready if you tap an ad button. This happens even if you never tap an ad button, but not if you have bought No Ads. The ad is never shown unless you tap.
  • If you have bought No Ads, the game does not contact Google's ad or consent services at all: there is no consent check, Google's ad library is not started and no ad is loaded. You get the rewards directly. Google Play Billing is still started, for your purchases (see below).

While the ad library is running, Google may collect and process information about your device, for example:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID) and other device identifiers,
  • IP address and an approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, language, operating system),
  • how you interact with the app and the ads (impressions and clicks),
  • diagnostic data, such as crash logs and performance.

Google uses this information to show ads, for ad analytics and measurement, and to prevent fraud. If you decline personalised ads, only ads that are not personalised to you are shown.

Changing your choice: You can change or withdraw your consent at any time with the Privacy options button under Settings (the gear at the top right of the game). The button is shown only where consent is required, and not if you have bought No Ads, since no ad data is processed then. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Privacy → Ads).

Learn more in Google's privacy policy and in how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-game purchases (Google Play)

The game is free to play. There are also optional purchases:

  • No Ads – every ad reward without watching ads, forever.
  • Starter Pack – a starter bundle that can be bought once.
  • Time Skip 4 h, 12 h and 24 h – in-game money right away (consumed, can be bought again).
  • Offline limit 8 h and 16 h – your cabs keep earning longer while you are away, forever.

The price is shown in Google Play before you buy. Purchases are made through Google Play Billing and payment is handled entirely by Google under Google's privacy policy. The game only learns which product was bought so that it can be delivered – we never receive your payment details. We have no server of our own, so purchases are not checked against any server of ours. Permanent purchases are tied to your Google account and can be restored with Restore purchases under Settings.

What we don't do

  • We have no accounts and no server of our own, and we receive no information about you.
  • The game contains no analytics or tracking tools of our own. The only things that collect information are Google's ad and consent services described above.
  • The game does not ask for permissions such as location, camera, contacts or files.
  • We do not sell any data.

Permissions

The game uses internet and network state (to contact Google's consent service, load ads and reach Google Play), the advertising ID (for AdMob, according to your consent), Google Play billing (for purchases), notifications (for reminders, only if you allow them) and vibration (a short buzz when you take a ride). Google's ad library and Android's library for background work also add technical permissions: Android's own ad services (Privacy Sandbox), keeping the device awake briefly and running short background work. None of them gives access to your location, your files or your contacts.

Legal basis

  • Personalised ads are shown only with your consent.
  • The consent check at start-up (for players who have not bought No Ads) is made so that we can ask for and respect your consent (legal obligation and legitimate interest).
  • Rewarded ads you choose to watch, non-personalised ads and purchases are processed to deliver what you chose in the game (contract).

Google is independently responsible for its own processing.

Retention

  • The game's data stays locally on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.
  • Any backup in your Google account remains until you delete it or Google deletes it under its own rules.
  • How long Google keeps information from ads, consent and purchases is decided by Google – see Google's information on retention.

Transfers outside the EU/EEA

Google may process information in countries outside the EU/EEA, for example in the United States. Google then relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, which Google LLC participates in, and on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Learn more about Google's data transfer frameworks.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access, correct and erase your personal data, to restrict processing, to object to processing and to withdraw your consent at any time. You change or withdraw your consent to ads with Privacy options under Settings in the game.

Since we receive no information about you, we have nothing to hand over or erase on our side. Information held by Google is managed in your Google account, or by contacting Google. If you have questions, you are welcome to email us.

If you think your data is being processed incorrectly, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), imy.se, or with the data protection authority where you live.

Children

The game is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your information

  • Everything the game has saved on the device is deleted if you clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall the game.
  • A backup in your Google account is deleted in Google Drive (under Backups) or in your phone's backup settings. Otherwise the save may come back if you reinstall the game.
  • Information held by Google is managed in your Google account and in Android's ad settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page with a new date.
