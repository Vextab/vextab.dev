Privacy policy for Såtid

Effective from 24 November 2026

In short: Såtid has no account, and Vextab receives no data from the app. Your journal is stored in the app on your phone and is included in Android's backup to your own Google account if you have backup turned on. The app fetches weather from SMHI, and the free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect data.

Who is responsible?

The app Såtid is developed by Vextab AB (reg. no. 559469-5446), Sweden, which is the data controller. Contact: support@vextab.dev.

No account and no server of our own

The app has no account and no sign-in. Vextab has no server of its own for the app and receives no data from it.

The app uses the internet to fetch the weather forecast from SMHI and, in the free version, to show ads from Google AdMob. Premium is purchased through Google Play. The sections below describe what is sent.

Your journal

Your notes, dates, harvests and photos are stored in the app's own storage on your phone. Vextab never receives them, and the app does not send them anywhere. If Android backup is turned on, they are included in it – see the section on backup.

You pick photos with the Android photo picker, so the app only gets the pictures you choose, or take them with the phone's camera app. The app has no permission to access your photos or the camera.

Weather from SMHI

The app fetches the weather forecast from SMHI, the Swedish Meteorological and Hydrological Institute (at opendata-download-metfcst.smhi.se). The request contains the coordinates of the town you have chosen in the app, with three decimals. These are the town's coordinates, not your phone's location.

No personal data is sent, but SMHI sees your phone's IP address, as every web service does. The latest forecast is stored on your phone and used for at most one hour.

Location (optional)

The app only requests approximate location, never precise location, and only when you tap Använd min position (“Use my location”) in the town picker. The location is obtained through the phone's location service (Google Play services) and is used on the phone to suggest the nearest town in the app's list. The app never stores the location and never sends it anywhere.

Android backup

If Android backup is turned on, the app's data – the journal with its photos and your settings – is included in the phone's backup to your own Google account, and is carried over when you move to a new phone. This is handled by Android and Google, not by Vextab, and can be turned off in the phone's settings.

Google has a limit of 25 MB per app. If the journal is larger, no backup is made.

Ads in the free version

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner and occasionally a full-screen ad. Premium removes all ads.

Before the first ad is shown, users in the EEA and the United Kingdom get to choose in Google's consent dialog. You can change your choice in the app under Inställningar → Integritetsval för reklam (Settings → “Privacy choices for ads”).

AdMob uses the phone's advertising ID and may collect data in accordance with Google's policy. Read more at Google: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Android's “Privacy Sandbox” permissions (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_*) are removed from the app.

Premium

Premium is a one-time purchase made through Google Play (Google Play Billing). The purchase is handled by Google Play. Vextab does not see or store any purchase or payment details.

Links

“Läs mer” (“Read more”) opens pages on sv.wikipedia.org in the phone's web browser. The app has no built-in browser.

Analytics and third-party services

The app has no analytics and no crash reporting beyond what AdMob does. These services from other companies are in the app, and no others:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob, with Google's consent dialog) – the ads in the free version.
  • Google Play Billing – the Premium purchase.
  • The location feature of Google Play services – only for “Använd min position”.

Android permissions

  • Internet (INTERNET) – for the weather forecast and the ads.
  • Approximate location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) – only when you tap “Använd min position”.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) – used by AdMob.
  • Purchases (BILLING) – the Premium purchase through Google Play.
  • WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE – used by the ads library, the first two for its background jobs.

The app has no permission for precise location, photos or the camera.

Sharing with third parties

Vextab shares no data with anyone, because we do not receive any. The services that the app or the phone contacts directly are SMHI (weather) and Google (ads, purchases, the location service, and backup if you have it turned on), as described in the sections above.

Deleting your information

The app's data is removed from the phone when you uninstall the app. A backup in your Google account may remain in accordance with Google's rules.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have, among other things, the right to know what data is processed about you and to have it corrected or erased. Because Vextab receives no data from the app, we have no such data to hand over, correct or erase. For data processed by Google, please contact Google.

You can always email us at support@vextab.dev. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

Children

Såtid is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy in the app? Email support@vextab.dev.
