Privacy policy for Min inköpslista

Effective from 22 November 2026

In short: Min inköpslista (“My shopping list”) does not collect, transmit or share any information about you. Everything you enter is stored only on your own device.

Who is responsible?

The app Min inköpslista is developed by Vextab AB (reg. no. 559469-5446), Sweden. Contact: info@vextab.dev.

What information does the app handle?

The app only handles what you enter yourself: items on your shopping list, saved lists and their names, your staple items and your theme choice (light/dark). This information is stored locally in the app on your device.

We do not collect any personal data. The app

  • has no account and no sign-in,
  • does not use the internet and sends no data anywhere,
  • contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking tools,
  • requests no permissions (such as location, camera, contacts or files).

Sharing, copying and backups

Some features let you move information out of the app yourself. This only happens when you choose to:

  • Share/copy the list: the list is handed to the app you choose (for example a messaging app) or to the clipboard. What happens next is governed by the receiving app.
  • Save backup: the app creates a file in the location you pick in the system file picker.
  • Restore from file: the app only reads the file you select.

Android's own backup

If backup is turned on in Android, the system may include the app's data in your own backup, for example in your Google account. This is handled by Android and Google under their terms – we have no access to it.

Sharing with third parties

We share no information with anyone, because we never receive any.

Deleting your information

You can delete items and lists directly in the app. All information in the app is deleted if you clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall the app.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy in the app? Email support@vextab.dev.
